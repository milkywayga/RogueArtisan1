The Craft

Crafted Slowly with Real Leather

Every piece from Rogue Artisan is handcrafted—cut, assembled, saddle-stitched, and finished by hand (unless otherwise specified). I work with full and top-grain leathers selected for their strength, natural texture, exciting colours, and ability to age gracefully.

No assembly lines. Just small-batch benchwork built to handle everyday use and pick up character along the way.

Leather Care & Maintenance
  • Natural Patina: Full-grain leather absorbs oils and exposure, darkening naturally into a rich patina over time.
  • Conditioning: Treat with a quality balm or beeswax conditioner every 3–6 months to keep the leather supple and weather-resistant.
  • Moisture & Drying: If exposed to water, wipe clean and allow to air dry naturally away from direct heat sources.
